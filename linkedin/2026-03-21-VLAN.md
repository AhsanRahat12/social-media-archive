VLANs let you split your local network into segments.

No extra cables. No extra hardware. Just configuration.

You can separate teams, isolate guest Wi-Fi, or carve out dedicated zones for sensitive systems, all on the same physical infrastructure.

If devices on different VLANs need to talk, traffic has to go through a router first. That's the point. It keeps things contained.

A compromised device stays in its VLAN. The blast radius stops there. The rest of your network carries on.

Same switches, same cables, completely different security posture.
