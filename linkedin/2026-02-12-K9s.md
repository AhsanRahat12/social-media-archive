# LinkedIn Post - k9s Discovery

I'm glad I started with kubectl before discovering k9s.

Spending weeks running kubectl commands manually during my KubeCraft internship built the foundation I needed. Now that I'm comfortable with the basics, k9s has become my productivity multiplier.

**The Progression:**

First, I learned kubectl the hard way:
```bash
kubectl get pods -n namespace-name
kubectl logs pod-name -n namespace-name | less
kubectl describe deployment deployment-name
```

This hands-on approach taught me exactly how Kubernetes resources work. No shortcuts, just raw commands until they became second nature.

**Then Came k9s:**

After building that kubectl foundation, k9s transformed my daily workflow with a terminal UI for Kubernetes.

Here's what boosted my productivity:

✅ Press `:pods`, `:deploy`, or `:service` to instantly switch between resource views (same operations, much faster navigation)

✅ Navigate to any pod and press 'l' for live logs, 's' for shell access, or 'y' to view YAML (one keystroke vs full commands)

✅ Sort by age (Shift + A) or status (Shift + S) to quickly spot recent deployments or failing resources

✅ Search logs with `/` followed by your search term (found critical errors in seconds)

✅ All commands visible on the top right corner of the screen (no need to memorize every keybinding)

**Real Impact:**

Debugging a failing deployment dropped from 5-6 separate kubectl commands to navigating k9s with keyboard shortcuts. Probably saves me 20-30 minutes daily.

**The Right Approach:**

Learn kubectl first. Build that foundation. Then use k9s for speed and efficiency in daily operations.
