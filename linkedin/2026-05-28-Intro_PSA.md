# LinkedIn Post 2 — What PSA Is and the 3 Levels

---

You do not need a third-party tool to start hardening Kubernetes pods.

Pod Security Admission is built directly into Kubernetes. Add one label to a namespace and Kubernetes enforces security rules on every pod inside it automatically.

Three levels exist:

1. Privileged: Zero restrictions. For system components only. Never your apps.
2. Baseline: Blocks the most dangerous misconfigurations. No privileged containers, no hostNetwork access.
3. Restricted: Heavily locked down. Enforces non-root, capability drops, and privilege escalation prevention on every pod automatically.

The most important thing to understand: you are not writing custom rules. Kubernetes already wrote them. You are choosing how strictly to apply them.

Most teams never apply any level at all. Their namespaces run with zero enforcement and they do not know it.

I just finished implementing PSA restricted on two production namespaces in my homelab. Full notes in the comments.

What level are your namespaces running at right now? 👇
