The most important principle I applied this week had nothing to do with Docker or Kubernetes.

It was separation of concerns, and it made my entire dev environment make sense.

Here's the idea:

✅ Personal tools and configs belong to you. They live in your dotfiles repo and follow you everywhere.
✅ Project tools belong to the team. They live in the project repo, scoped to that directory only.
✅ Neither should know the other exists.

Before I understood this, my configs were a mess. Personal tools bleeding into project setups. Team environments breaking because of my own preferences.

Once I drew a hard line between the two, everything became predictable, portable, and clean.

The same principle that makes good software architecture makes a good dev environment.
