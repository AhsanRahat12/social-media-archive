
When I started tinkering with Kubernetes in my home lab, the number of distros available was overwhelming.
After digging deeper into each one, I landed on K3S. Here is why:

- Kubeadm is great for the CKA exam but long term you are manually managing certificates and CNI plugins. Not ideal for a stable home lab.
- MicroK8S installs easily but uses its own microk8s kubectl command instead of standard kubectl, and it is optimized for Ubuntu. I run Arch Linux, so that was a non starter.
- Talos Linux is production grade and genuinely impressive. But it locks you out of the OS completely. I wanted to understand everything from the ground up, and I felt taking that big a step this early would hamper my learning journey.
- K3S installs with one command, runs on a Raspberry Pi, lets you troubleshoot at the OS level, and powers Rancher in enterprise environments.

The deciding factor was simple.
Which one would teach me the most while still being production relevant.
K3S won.
