I write about how Renovate changed how I handle container image updates in my home lab. I used to manually check Docker Hub and release pages every few days just in case something important shipped. Now Renovate watches my image tags and opens a PR when something updates. I review the diff, merge, Flux deploys it.
3 to 4 PRs a month instead of me guessing what's outdated.
