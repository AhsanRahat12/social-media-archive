When I started tinkering with Kubernetes in my home lab, the number of distros available was overwhelming.
After digging deeper I landed on K3S.
Here is why every other option lost 🧵

Tweet 2
Kubeadm is what the CKA exam uses.
Great for learning Kubernetes deeply. But long term you are manually managing certificates and CNI plugins.
Not what I wanted for a stable home lab.

Tweet 3
MicroK8S installs easily.
But it uses microk8s kubectl instead of standard kubectl and it is optimized for Ubuntu.
I run Arch Linux. That was a non starter.

Tweet 4
Talos Linux is genuinely impressive. Production grade, hardened, immutable.
But it locks you out of the OS completely. No SSH. No poking around.
I wanted to understand everything from the ground up. Taking that step too early would have hampered my journey.

Tweet 5
K3S installs with one command.
Runs on a Raspberry Pi. Lets you SSH in and troubleshoot at the OS level. Powers Rancher in enterprise environments.
Best balance of simplicity and real learning.

Tweet 6
The deciding factor was simple.
Which distro would teach me the most while still being production relevant.
K3S won.
