# Post 02 — GitOps (X / Twitter)

---

**Tweet 1**
I deleted a resource from my cluster. Flux put it right back.

Here is why that changes everything about how you manage infrastructure 🧵

---

**Tweet 2**
Before GitOps everything was manual.

kubectl apply. Tweak directly in the cluster. No record of what changed or why.

It works. Until something breaks and you have no idea what you did.

---

**Tweet 3**
After GitOps your Git repo is your cluster.

Flux watches your repo and makes the cluster match it. Always.

You stop managing the cluster directly. You manage Git.

---

**Tweet 4**
What that actually means in practice:

Delete something by accident, Flux restores it.
Bad change merged, `git revert` is your rollback.
New cluster, point Flux at the same repo and it rebuilds everything.

---

**Tweet 5**
The config lives in Git, not in the cluster.

That distinction sounds small. It is not.

Are you running GitOps yet?
