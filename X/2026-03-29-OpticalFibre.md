Most people in DevOps and cloud never think about fibre. But they should.

Not all fibre is the same. There are two types:

→ Multi-mode: wide core, light bounces around inside it. Works great up to ~550m. Cheaper, easier to work with. Perfect inside data centres and campus buildings.

→ Single-mode: narrow core, light travels in a straight line. Goes 100km+ without signal loss. Used for long-haul runs, city-to-city, even undersea cables.

Wrong choice for your setup = expensive mistake.
