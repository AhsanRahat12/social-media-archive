1/ About to add health checks to a few services in my home lab. Before touching any YAML, wanted to actually understand what I was configuring instead of copy-pasting a probe block.

2/ Liveness and readiness probes sound like the same thing. They're not, and mixing them up is an easy mistake.

3/ Liveness: is this process still alive, or should it be restarted? Fails enough times, pod gets killed and replaced.

4/ Readiness: is this pod actually able to serve traffic right now? Fails, pod stays alive, Kubernetes just stops routing to it until it passes again.

5/ The part that took a minute to click: a process can be technically running (liveness passes) while completely unable to do its job, still booting, waiting on a DB connection. Skip readiness and that half-broken pod eats live traffic the whole time.

6/ Now I actually know what I'm writing before I write it. Liveness decides restart, readiness decides routing.
