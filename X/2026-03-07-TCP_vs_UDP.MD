TCP and UDP both transport data. They make completely opposite bets about what matters most.

TCP bets on reliability:
→ Establishes a connection before sending anything
→ Numbers every segment for correct ordering
→ Retransmits anything that gets lost
→ Controls flow so the receiver is not overwhelmed

UDP bets on speed:
→ No handshake, no sequencing, no retransmission
→ Fires data and moves on
→ Lost packets stay lost

When UDP wins:
→ Video streaming. A dropped frame beats buffering.
→ Online gaming. A delayed position update is worse than a wrong one.
→ DNS. A quick question and answer needs no full connection setup.
→ VoIP. A tiny audio glitch beats a noticeable delay.

The choice is never TCP good, UDP bad. It is always: what does this use case actually need?
