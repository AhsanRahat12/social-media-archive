Have you ever wondered how the internet knows how to get data from one side of the world to the other?

It's BGP, Border Gateway Protocol, and most people have no idea it exists.

The internet is made up of thousands of independent networks. Your ISP is one. Google is one. Amazon is one. BGP is how they all talk to each other and agree on how to route traffic.

The scary part? It's built on trust. A single misconfigured announcement can reroute huge chunks of internet traffic. And it has happened before.

One protocol. Quietly running everything.
