The most important principle I applied this week had nothing to do with Docker or Kubernetes.

It was separation of concerns.

✅ Personal tools live in your dotfiles repo, they follow you everywhere.
✅ Project tools live in the project repo, scoped to the team only.
✅ Neither should know the other exists.

One principle. Cleaner configs. No more broken environments.

Full setup on my GitHub: github.com/AhsanRahat12
