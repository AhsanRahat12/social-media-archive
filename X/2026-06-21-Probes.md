**Tweet 1 / 7**

🧵 Do you actually know why you added that liveness probe?

I run self-hosted apps on Kubernetes. Audiobookshelf, Linkding, Grafana. Apps I didn't write, so I work with whatever health endpoints they expose.

Before configuring any probe, I ask four questions:

---

**Tweet 2 / 7**

1. Can the app break while the process is still running?

Crashed database connection, deadlocked thread, corrupted file. Process alive, app dead. Kubernetes won't know without a liveness probe.

---

**Tweet 3 / 7**

2. Does it receive traffic and need time to become ready?

Without a readiness probe, requests hit the pod before it can handle them.

---

**Tweet 4 / 7**

3. Does it start slowly?

If yes, startup probe. Buys the app time to initialise without the liveness probe killing it before it's even ready.

---

**Tweet 5 / 7**

4. Is it managed by an operator?

The operator likely handles health checking already. Check before adding your own.

---

**Tweet 6 / 7**

For my apps the answers were: yes, yes, no, no.

Liveness probe. Readiness probe. No startup probe.

Two questions answered. Two probes configured. No guessing.
