You hit send to someone on your local network. Feels instant. Here is what actually happens:

→ Your machine listens before sending anything
→ Medium is clear? It wraps data into a frame with source + destination MAC
→ Two devices send at the same time? Collision detected, both stop, wait randomly, retry
→ Frame carries a checksum at the end — corrupted on arrival? Silently dropped

This is how local machines actually communicate. No IPs involved. No internet. Just two devices, frames, and MAC addresses.

Most people never think about this layer. DevOps engineers should.
