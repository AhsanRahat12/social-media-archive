Every device in your home has its own IP. Your ISP gives you exactly one public IP.

So how are dozens of devices online at the same time? NAT.

The trick is ports:
→ Phone sends a request. Router swaps private IP for public. Assigns port 60001.
→ Laptop does the same. Same public IP, port 60002.
→ TV does the same. Same public IP, port 60003.

Response comes back on port 60001? Router knows exactly where to send it.

One public IP. Every device tracked individually. The internet sees one address. Your router handles the rest.

This is also why unsolicited inbound connections cannot reach devices behind NAT. No outbound request means no mapping means no way in.
